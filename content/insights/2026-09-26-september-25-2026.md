- **Leader — Learn:** GKE's doubled pod-density ceiling may shift cluster sizing and cost economics for teams running dense workloads; the new X5 48TB memory tier is worth noting only if the org operates SAP HANA at scale and previously hit the 32TB single-node ceiling.

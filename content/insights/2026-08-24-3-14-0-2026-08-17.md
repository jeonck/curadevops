- **Platform/SRE — Plan:** Three behavioral changes affect running Prometheus deployments: the stats query-parameter deprecation (other values still work but will be rejected in the next major), the __meta_hetzner_datacenter label drop for hcloud targets, and PromQL duration expressions now enabled by default. Review your relabeling configs, any Hetzner service-discovery rules, and PromQL queries before upgrading; no hard deadline yet since rejection is deferred to the next major release.

- **Platform/SRE — Act:** The eMudhra intermediate CA private keys are confirmed compromised, enabling potential MITM against any service trusting those CAs; upgrade Envoy to v1.40.1 now to remove trust in those CAs from the proxy trust store. If running Istio, also review the peer metadata label-fallback behavior change, which affects source_app/destination_app attribution in telemetry.

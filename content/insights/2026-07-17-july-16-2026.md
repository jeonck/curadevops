- **Platform/SRE — Plan:** Apigee X instances below 1-17-0-apigee-10 with maintenance windows configured will be auto-updated within 7–21 days of July 16; verify now that your instances don't carry the DNS misconfiguration (known issue 445936920) or a dependency on the removed Apigee Java Library, as either will block the automatic update.

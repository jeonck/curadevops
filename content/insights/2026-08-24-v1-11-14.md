- **Platform/SRE — Act:** OpenTofu 1.11 hit EOL on 2026-08-19 and this is its final patch; the credential-leak via OCI HTTP redirect and the DoS via crafted remote-state URLs are both active security risks in IaC runs. Upgrade to a supported OpenTofu release series (1.12+) now that EOL has passed.
- **CI/CD — Act:** The credential-leak bug affects `tofu init` when pulling modules or providers from OCI registries — a standard pipeline step — and could expose registry credentials to a redirect target. Upgrade the OpenTofu version pinned in CI pipelines from 1.11.x to a supported series immediately; 1.11 is already EOL.
